Our Story

Handmade in Portsaid, made for your moments.

Crafty Gifts started as a small home-based craft project in Portsaid, Egypt, making personalized keepsakes for family and friends. What began with a handful of engraved plaques has grown into a shop trusted for graduation gifts, newborn keepsakes, and custom pieces made for every kind of celebration.

Every piece is designed and finished by hand — from the first sketch of your name or message, to the final engrave, to the moment it's wrapped and ready to give. We believe a gift means more when it's made specifically for the person receiving it, not pulled off a shelf.
